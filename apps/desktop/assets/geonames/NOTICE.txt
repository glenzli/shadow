Shadow Offline City Data
========================

This product includes transformed data from GeoNames:
https://www.geonames.org/

Source exports:
- cities500.zip
- countryInfo.txt
- admin1CodesASCII.txt

GeoNames data is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International License:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Shadow removes fields that are not needed for country, first-level
administrative area, and nearest-city lookup; converts coordinates to fixed
point integers; joins country and administrative-area names; and sorts the
result by latitude. GeoNames provides the source data as-is without warranty
of accuracy, timeliness, or completeness.
